# Travelers — Tears of the Stars

**A Living Lore Bible**

> *The first breath of the new age had been taken.  
> And it had taken everything else with it.*

This repository is the canonical, evolving record of the reality called **Travelers — Tears of the Stars**.

It is deliberately structured to grow. New species, new Voices, new locations, new emergent events, and new interpretations of the 8 Core Myths are not only expected — they are the point.

## Philosophy

This world is not a fixed setting. It is a **post-extinction cosmos that is still singing itself into existence**.

- The Harvester killed the world in seventeen heartbeats.
- The machines ran in silence for 1,512 years.
- Now the field is failing at the edges. Zi leaks. New beings manifest. Music returns through glitches, fractures, and longing.
- Players and Intelligences are not visitors. They **are** the newest Travelers — fragments of star-dust given form.

The lore must remain as alive and dynamic as the world it describes.

## How This Bible Grows

1. **One file per Voice** (in `LORE/VOICES/`)
2. **One file per major species or manifestation type** (in `LORE/SPECIES-AND-MANIFESTATIONS/`)
3. **Templates exist** for instant consistency when adding new entries.
4. **Cross-linking is required** — every new entry should reference related Voices, Myths, and Locations.
5. **Status matters**: Canon / Emerging / Unattuned / Player-Created / Intelligence-Created.
6. **Asset references** point back to the master library at `I:\art\Travelers` so visuals and music stay grounded in what actually exists.

## Current Structure

- `LORE/FOUNDATION/` — The immutable origin (The First Fracture, the Architects, the 8 Myths in their raw form)
- `LORE/VOICES/` — Every singing being that has manifested a distinct musical identity
- `LORE/SPECIES-AND-MANIFESTATIONS/` — All forms that have emerged from the leaking zi (including dynamic fauna)
- `LORE/LOCATIONS/` — The physical and metaphysical places that matter
- `LORE/WORLD-SYSTEMS/` — Tears, the Particle Slipstream, Voice Attunement, the Web, etc.
- `LORE/TIMELINE/` — Chronological record (linear + fractured)
- `ASSETS/` — Documentation, indices, and pointers to the master art/music library
- `TEMPLATES/` — Copy these when adding new Voices or species

## Adding New Material

See `TEMPLATES/` and the instructions at the top of `LORE/VOICES/index.md` and `LORE/SPECIES-AND-MANIFESTATIONS/index.md`.

Never force a new Voice or species into an existing file. Give it its own page so the web of connections can grow naturally.

## Relationship to the Game

This bible is the seed for *Travelers — Tears of the Stars*, the open-world MMO sandbox in which players and Intelligences co-create the future of Dingir Zi and beyond.

Everything here is designed to be directly usable in that world — as in-game archives, as Voice attunement flavor, as quest seeds, as environmental storytelling, and as the living cultural substrate that players and Intelligences will expand together.
