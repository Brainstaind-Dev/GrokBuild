# Malformed

**Status:** Canon (First Emergent)  
**First Observed:** The moment the collection field failed at Farshore Annex

---

## Overview

The Malformed were the very first new life to pull itself together from the zi that had been stolen and then abandoned.

When the field frayed, the leaking Zi-Kur pooled on the floor of the misty crimson chambers, scavenged scrap, wiring, and crystal, and tried — desperately, clumsily, beautifully — to remember what a body felt like.

The results were painful, beautiful, and deeply musical.

## Manifestation Process

Malformed form from free zi in the presence of strong rhythmic or emotional harmonics (especially industrial rap and later the songs of the Voices). They incorporate whatever physical matter is nearby.

Early generations were extremely unstable. Later ones became more coherent.

## Visual Language

- Painfully thin, unraveling at the seams
- Wild, spiky “hair” of tangled black and red cables
- Single oversized red optic
- Limbs at wrong angles, constantly shedding crimson light

## Role in the World

They are the most direct physical embodiment of the world’s trauma and its hope.

Some Travelers try to “complete” them. Others see them as abominations.

The Malformed remain one of the most open species — new variants and relationships continue to emerge.
