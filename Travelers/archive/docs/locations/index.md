# Locations

The places that have shaped (and continue to shape) the post-Fracture world.

---

## Major Known Locations

- **The Central Spire & Ziggurat of Eternal Measures** — Heart of the old Architect power.
- **Farshore Annex (Peripheral Relay Station 19)** — The forgotten edge where the field first failed. Birthplace of the first glitch and the first Malformed.
- **The Pink Shore** — The liminal beach of pink flora under the blood-red sky. The most iconic image of the awakened world.
- **Toroidal Rings & Crimson Tube Facilities** — The vast storage architecture that still holds most of the world’s stolen zi.
- **The Central Harvester Node** — The endgame. The strongest remaining section of the array.
- **Ruined Biomechanical Cities** — Former habitations of the Architects, now inhabited by new life.

---

*Every location in this world is either dying, being reborn, or both.*
