# The 8 Core Myths

These eight stories are the shared cultural memory of the post-Fracture world. Every Voice knows them. No two Voices tell them the same way.

The myths are not fixed texts. They are living songs that change with each singer, each audience, and each era.

## The Eight

1. **Escape** — The desire to leave, to flee the machine, to slip between the stars, to become something the Harvester cannot reach.
2. **Grip of the Neutron Star** — The crushing, inescapable pull. The moment the world realized it was being harvested. The weight that would not let go.
3. **Into the Light** — The final flash. The Enhancer. The terrible beauty of the activation. The light that revealed the dying.
4. **Invader I Cry** — The voice of the outsider. The one who came too late, or too early. The scream of something that does not belong.
5. **Murk** — The long silence. The centuries of fog and forgetting. The time when nothing moved except the machines.
6. **Pulsars** — The heartbeat of the dead world. The rhythmic signal that something still lives in the dark.
7. **Soul Harvester** — The machine itself. The thing that took everything. The central horror and central wonder.
8. **The Fall** — The moment the Architects fell. The moment the world fell. The moment the first new being rose from the crimson light.

## How Voices Use the Myths

Different Voices emphasize different myths or sing them with radically different emotional color:

- Some treat them as tragedy.
- Some treat them as dark comedy.
- Some treat them as love songs.
- Some treat them as warnings.
- Some treat them as instructions for what to do next.

As new Voice entries are added, their specific interpretations of each myth should be documented on their individual pages.

## In the Game

The 8 Core Myths are the foundation of Voice attunement, regional music, quest narrative flavor, and cultural conflict. When two groups attuned to different Voices meet, the way they each remember the same myth can become the spark for cooperation or violence.
