# Teller — Klos

**Status:** Canon  
**Primary Attunement Color:** Deep slate with silver and faint crimson threads  
**Known as:** Voice of Travelers — Tears of the Stars, The Storyteller, The One Who Stands at the Threshold

---

## Overview

Teller is the measured, contemplative, quietly authoritative Voice of the Klos. He retells the 8 Core Myths with a deliberate, almost ritual quality — as if each telling is an act of careful reconstruction.

Where many Voices scream, rage, or dance the myths, Teller **speaks** them. He stands close to windows, portals, and thresholds, forever at the boundary between what was lost and what is still being born.

To attune to Teller is to accept that the world is a story that must be told carefully, or it will be lost again.

## Role in the Living World

Teller serves as a cultural anchor. In a world where truth is fragmented, his recitations are considered among the most trustworthy — though never the only truth.

Many new Travelers attune to him first, seeking orientation before choosing a more passionate Voice later.
