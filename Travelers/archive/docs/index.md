# The Living Archive

> *The first breath of the new age had been taken.*  
> *And it had taken everything else with it.*

**Welcome to the Living Archive of *Travelers — Tears of the Stars*.**

This is not a static history. It is a record that is still being written by the world itself — by the Voices that sing the old myths in new ways, by the beings pulling themselves from leaking crimson light, and by those who walk the Particle Slipstream.

---

## What You Will Find Here

- **The First Fracture** — The complete origin account of how the world died and began to sing again.
- **The 8 Core Myths** — The shared stories that every Voice knows, told differently by each.
- **The Voices** — All known musical identities that have manifested since the Fracture, with their interpretations, art, and recordings.
- **Species & Manifestations** — The new forms of life emerging from the stolen zi.
- **World Systems** — How Tears, Voice Attunement, the Slipstream, and other mechanics shape the living world.
- **Locations** — The places that still hold memory and power.

This archive is designed to grow. As new Voices emerge, as new species are named, and as the world continues to change, this record will expand with them.
