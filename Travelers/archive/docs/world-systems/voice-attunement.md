# Voice Attunement

**The Personal Soundtrack and Narrative Lens of Every Traveler**

---

## What It Is

When a new Traveler finishes their journey through the Particle Slipstream, they may choose to **attune** to one of the known Voices.

This choice is fundamentally atmospheric, narrative, and cultural.

From the moment of attunement onward:

- The Traveler hears the world through that Voice’s music
- Regional and event music shifts to reflect their attunement
- Narration and flavor take on the emotional color of that Voice
- Others react differently based on cultural reputation

## Why It Matters

In a world where the 8 Core Myths have no single correct version, the Voice you attune to is the version of reality you have chosen to walk inside.

Two Travelers attuned to different Voices can experience the exact same event and come away with radically different emotional truths.

This is not a bug. This is the point.

## Changing Attunement

It is possible (though not trivial) to change Voices later. This usually requires significant narrative weight — a profound experience, a betrayal, or a long pilgrimage.
