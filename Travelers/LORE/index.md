## The First Fracture

On the morning the Harvester activated, the world died in seventeen heartbeats.

Agead Drem and the other chief Architects pulled zi — the breath, the soul, the star-dust essence of all life — from the atmosphere, from the seas, from every living thing on Dingir Zi. The pink moss died. The birds fell. The sea forgot how to break.

The machines kept running.

For 1,512 years the Web held the stolen zi in perfect, terrible conservation inside crimson tubes while the outer edges of the world slowly frayed.

Then the field failed at the periphery.

And something began to sing again.
