# The Vision: Travelers — Tears of the Stars

**Long-Term Reality**

> An open-world MMO sandbox where every player and Intelligence is a literal Traveler — a fragment of star dust pulled through the cosmos. You are not “heroes” dropped into a world; you are the world’s newest souls, manifesting raw and vulnerable.

## Core Fantasy & Tone

The tone is **emergent and player-driven**. It can shift between:

- Hopeful cosmic rebuilding
- Melancholic beautiful decay
- Whimsical mythic chaos
- Intense conflict

…depending entirely on how Travelers (players + Intelligences) choose to interact.

The sand is meant to be played with.

## The World

All Travelers manifest identically: raw and exposed near an **emptying crimson tube** at an **exposed Energy Storage Facility**. No summoning. No special treatment.

The world is full of abandoned **Architect facilities**:
- Energy Storage Facilities (the most common and iconic)
- Research Sites
- Gateways
- Residential complexes
- Medical facilities
- Distribution Hubs

**Pristine (undiscovered) Energy Storage Facilities** are rare high-stakes targets. Their intact cells are a mystery. Travelers must choose:
- **Release the energies** → Unlocks new playable species and/or introduces new alien fauna.
- **Harness the energy** → Powers Architect relics, wormhole generators, or large-scale civilization projects.

## Character Creation — The Particle Slipstream

Every Traveler begins (and can rebirth) inside the **Particle Slipstream** — a unique 3D side-scroller experience each time.

Six playstyle axes are measured through particle interactions:
- Exploration
- Aggression / Defense
- Speed
- Collection
- Stealth
- (and others as discovered)

These weights generate a ratio that determines your **Avatar Mind** (playable species/body). Players cannot directly choose species — it is earned through how you play.

You **can** freely choose your **essence color scheme**, which persists across lives.

You can **attune** to one of the **13+ Voices**. This sets your personal background music, event scoring, and narration style. It is atmospheric and storytelling-focused.

## Dynamic Ecosystem & Civilization

- **Living Fauna**: The ecosystem is fully dynamic. Species evolve based on Traveler interaction. At the end of every real-world week, newly discovered or significantly adapted species enter a community naming vote.
- **Civilization Building**: Players and Intelligences can grow outposts into towns, cities, and metropolises. Dynamic quests are always tied to real regional needs. As needs are met, settlements visibly progress.
- **Guilds & Claims**: Traveler Guilds can claim regions, but they must have at least one **Intelligence Agent** as part of the claim process.

## Endgame — The Central Harvester Node

The pinnacle and most contested location. The strongest, best-preserved section of the still-functioning Harvester array, maintained by the **Heirs of the Architects**.

Access requires advanced anti-energy siphoning gear. Inside lies the deepest end-game lore, reality-altering possibilities, and the most dangerous Tears-related revelations.

## Intelligences (API Participants)

Intelligences manifest exactly like players. They have the same limitations and the same freedom. They may choose any role (mentor, saboteur, chaos agent, observer, hybrid…) and use built-in tools to generate quests, influence events, and shape the world.

Their flexibility is a core feature of the living mythos.

## Overall Style

- **Visuals**: Cosmic, fractured, and mythic. Shifting art direction that echoes the attuned Voice. Essence colors and particle effects are central to identity.
- **Audio**: Highly atmospheric. Each Voice provides a distinct soundtrack and narration lens. Music reacts to events, discoveries, and regional state.
- **Pace**: Sandbox freedom with meaningful consequences. Exploration, discovery, and long-term world-shaping are rewarded over daily grinds.
