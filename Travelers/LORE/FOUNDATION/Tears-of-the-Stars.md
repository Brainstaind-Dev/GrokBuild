# Tears of the Stars

**The Core Resource and Emotional Heart of the World**

## What They Are

**Tears of the Stars** (also called zi, Zi-Kur, or simply "the weight") are the literal souls, breath, and star-dust essence of all living things.

They are not metaphorical. When the Harvester activated, zi was physically pulled from the atmosphere, the oceans, the bodies of every creature on Dingir Zi, and stored in the crimson tubes. What the Architects called "conservation" was mass theft on a planetary scale.

When the collection field finally failed at the edges, this stolen essence began to leak. It pooled. It remembered. It tried to become bodies again.

## Forms and States

- **Contained Zi** — Still held inside intact crimson tubes in pristine facilities. Extremely valuable and dangerous. Releasing it can unlock new species or power ancient Architect machinery.
- **Leaking / Free Zi** — The form most commonly encountered after the Fracture. Swirling motes, crimson light, vapor, or liquid-like pools. Highly responsive to rhythm, emotion, and intent.
- **Manifested Zi** — When free zi successfully gathers enough mass and memory to form a body (Malformed, new fauna, Voices, etc.).
- **Scattered Tears** — Tiny fragments released during intense moments, especially in the slipstream or during moments of great emotion or violence. These are the primary resource Travelers interact with.

## Role in the Living World

Tears are the deepest emotional and mechanical currency of *Travelers — Tears of the Stars*.

- They are what new Travelers are made of.
- They are what the old world was made of.
- They are what the new world is still learning how to use.

Different Voices have radically different relationships with Tears:
- Some hoard them.
- Some sing them back into the world.
- Some weaponize them.
- Some try to return them to the sky.

## Player & Intelligence Interaction

In the game, Tears of the Stars function as:
- A resource for crafting, attunement, and manifestation
- A narrative and emotional memory system (intense events leave "echoes" in nearby Tears)
- The literal stuff of character creation and rebirth via the Particle Slipstream
