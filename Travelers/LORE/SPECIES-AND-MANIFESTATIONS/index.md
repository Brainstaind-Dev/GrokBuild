# Species and Manifestations

**Everything that has pulled itself together from the leaking zi since the First Fracture.**

Not every being that emerged became a Voice. Many are still learning to sing. Some may never do so. Others are still forming.

This section documents all forms of life — sapient, semi-sapient, and purely ecological — that now exist in the broken world.

**To add a new species or major manifestation type:**
1. Copy `TEMPLATES/SPECIES-TEMPLATE.md`
2. Create the new file in this folder
3. Update this index
4. Add cross-references from any related Voice pages

## Dynamic Fauna & Ecosystem

The non-sapient (or not-yet-sapient) life of the world is fully responsive to Traveler and Intelligence action.

- Over-harvesting, domestication, protection, or neglect all change the ecosystem over time.
- At the end of every real-world week, significantly adapted or newly discovered fauna enter a **community naming vote**.
- The first finder proposes a name. The population votes. Majority wins.

**Current dynamic fauna entries will be added here as they are documented in play.**

## Unclassified or Fragmentary Manifestations

- Various unnamed forms still pulling themselves from the oldest leaking tubes
- "Echoes" — temporary, unstable shapes that appear near intense concentrations of Tears during emotional events
- Hybrid forms that incorporate elements of multiple species

These are recorded in the [Emergent Log](../TIMELINE/Emergent-Log.md) until they stabilize enough for their own entry.
