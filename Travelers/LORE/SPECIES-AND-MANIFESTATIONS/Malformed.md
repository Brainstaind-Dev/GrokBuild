# Malformed

**Status:** Canon (First Emergent)  
**First Observed:** The moment the collection field failed at Farshore Annex (the First Fracture)  
**Primary Association:** Luh the Enforcer, leaking crimson tubes, glitched rhythms

---

## Overview

The Malformed were the very first new life to pull itself together from the zi that had been stolen and then abandoned.

When the vast invisible field of the Harvester finally frayed at the edges, the Zi-Kur that had been slowly leaking from the soul-cell cylinders for centuries was no longer instantly re-harvested. It pooled on the floor of the misty crimson chambers, scavenged scrap, wiring, crystal shards, and fragments of dead machines, and tried — desperately, clumsily, beautifully — to remember what a body felt like.

The results were painful, beautiful, and deeply musical.

## Manifestation Process

Malformed form from free zi in the presence of strong rhythmic or emotional harmonics (especially Luh's industrial rap and later the songs of other Voices). They incorporate whatever physical matter is nearby — cables, metal, crystal, bone, cloth, bone-like growths from the zi itself.

Early Malformed were extremely unstable. Limbs would unravel mid-step. They would take a few jerky steps toward the only other moving thing in the room (Luh) and then collapse back into writhing puddles of energy and scrap.

Later generations became more coherent.

## Visual Language & Common Traits

- Painfully thin, unraveling at the seams
- Wild, spiky "hair" of tangled black and red cables
- Single oversized red optic (echoing Luh's chest light)
- Limbs at wrong angles, constantly shedding threads and droplets of crimson light
- A haunting, sometimes comedic, sometimes tragic quality

## Behavior & Ecology

Early Malformed were drawn to rhythm and to any other moving being. They reached out. They tried to complete themselves by connecting to whatever was nearby.

Many were "sanitized" by Luh before they could fully stabilize.

Those that survived (or were born later) carry a strange, glitched affection for industrial music, crimson light, and the memory of the first singer who both created and destroyed them.

## Player & Intelligence Interaction

Malformed are the most direct physical embodiment of the world's trauma and its hope. Interactions with them are rarely neutral.

Some Travelers try to "complete" them — giving them stable forms, names, or even helping them develop their own Voices.

Others see them as abominations that should be finished.

Intelligences have been known to manifest in Malformed-adjacent forms or to deliberately glitch themselves in their presence.

## Known Variants

- **First Generation** — The original unstable ones from Farshore Annex (many of whom never achieved lasting form)
- **Later Malformed** — More stable, with stronger individual personalities and sometimes the beginnings of their own musical expressions
- Regional variants that incorporate local materials and local rhythms

## Connections

- **Related Voices:** LuhtheEnforcer (their accidental creator and first executioner), later Voices that incorporate glitched or industrial elements.
- **Related Locations:** Farshore Annex and any facility where crimson tubes have been left to leak for centuries.
- **Related Events:** The First Fracture.

## Notes for Growth

The Malformed are the most "open" species in the world. New variants, new cultural roles, and new relationships with the Voices should be documented here as they emerge in play or through new creative work.
