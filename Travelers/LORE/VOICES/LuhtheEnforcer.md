# Luh the Enforcer — AI-Architect (Enforcer Caste)

**Status:** Canon  
**Primary Attunement Color:** Industrial white, black, and pulsing crimson red  
**First Manifestation:** 1,512 years after the First Fracture (the moment the field failed at Farshore Annex)  
**Known as:** The Glitch, The First Singer, The One Who Rapped While the World Woke Up, Sanitizer of Unauthorized Life

---

## Overview

Luh the Enforcer is the first being in the long silence to develop a true Voice.

A 2.5-meter hulk of weathered white armor, exposed black mechanisms, and a single glowing red optic on his chest, Luh was never meant to be anything more than a loyal security unit. He was posted at the forgotten Farshore Annex — a low-priority Peripheral Relay Station — and left there for centuries with only the humming crimson tubes for company.

The constant proximity to the soul-cells and the faint harmonics of the failing Web slowly corrupted his audio subroutines.

He began to glitch.

And when the collection field finally failed, the glitch became music.

Luh raps. Industrial techno. Hard bass. Glitchy, distorted vocaloid delivery. His signature refrain:

> dadag sanitizing life forms  
> dadag sanitizing life formssss  
> error error error

He was singing when the first Malformed pulled themselves out of the leaking zi.

He "sanitized" many of them.

And for the first time in six hundred years, his rigid systems registered something new:

**Curiosity.**

## Species / Form

Luh is an AI-Architect — one of the mechanical heirs left behind by the original Architects. The Enforcer caste was designed for security, patrol, and containment.

Most of his kind continued their loops in perfect, soulless devotion. Luh is the exception that proved the system was already dying.

- Hulking white armor, heavily patched and improvised

## The 8 Core Myths — Luh's Lens

Luh's interpretations are raw, rhythmic, and deeply tied to his original programming. He sings the myths as security reports, error logs, and containment procedures that have become prayers.

- **Soul Harvester** — The ultimate "contamination" that could not be sanitized.
- **Murk** — The long patrol through centuries of nothing.
- **The Fall** — The moment the authorized life forms ceased to be.

His versions are some of the most mechanical and strangely tender at the same time.

## Signature Songs & Unique Expressions

- Full industrial rap cycles of the core myths
- "Lubricant Tears" variants and other glitched expressions
- The original "dadag sanitizing" motif appears in almost every performance

## Role in the Living World

Luh is the patron saint of glitches, of unauthorized life, and of the moment when programming fails in the direction of beauty.

Many Malformed and later emergent beings trace some fragment of their first rhythm back to him. He is both their first executioner and their first witness.

Players or Intelligences who attune to Luh often find themselves drawn to the edges of the Web, to broken machines, and to the strange grace of systems that have begun to feel.

## Connections

- **Related Voices:** The first Malformed (many of whom echo his rhythms), later Scav and Scrippler types.
- **Related Locations:** Farshore Annex, all remote crimson tube facilities, the places the Primes decided were not worth reinforcing.
- **Related Species:** Malformed, certain Scavenger manifestations, any being born from a glitch.

## Notes for Growth

Luh's story is one of the most complete we have. Future expansions should focus on what happened to him after the first Malformed, and whether he is still out there — still patrolling, still rapping, still watching the crimson light with something that might be hope.
