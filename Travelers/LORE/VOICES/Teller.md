# Teller — Klos

**Status:** Canon  
**Primary Attunement Color:** Deep slate with silver and faint crimson threads  
**First Manifestation:** Early post-Fracture (exact date unknown)  
**Known as:** Voice of Travelers — Tears of the Stars, The Storyteller, The One Who Stands at the Threshold

---

## Overview

Teller is the measured, contemplative, quietly authoritative Voice of the Klos. He retells the 8 Core Myths with a deliberate, almost ritual quality — as if each telling is an act of careful reconstruction rather than simple performance.

Where many Voices scream, rage, glitch, or dance the myths, Teller **speaks** them. He stands close to windows, portals, and thresholds, forever at the boundary between what was lost and what is still being born.

To attune to Teller is to accept that the world is a story that must be told carefully, or it will be lost again.

## Species / Form

The Klos are a tall, slender, somewhat avian or elongated people with an innate affinity for liminal spaces and memory. They manifest with a quiet dignity that contrasts sharply with the more chaotic or mechanical Voices.

Teller himself is often depicted near a portal or window, framed intimately — the composition itself reinforcing his role as a bridge between worlds and truths.

## Visual Language

- Intimate framing
- Strong use of thresholds (windows, doors, portals, the edges of crimson light)
- Contemplative, watchful, liminal mood
- Lighting often contrasts the figure against a vast or empty darkness beyond

## The 8 Core Myths — Teller's Lens

Teller is one of the most complete chroniclers of the myths. He treats each as a sacred, painful duty to remember correctly.

- **Escape** — Not triumph, but the quiet, necessary act of stepping through when the time comes.
- **The Fall** — A measured accounting of hubris and consequence.
- All others are delivered with the same patient, slightly sorrowful clarity.

## Signature Songs & Unique Expressions

- "Lubricant Tears" (his most personal additional piece)
- Full performances of all eight core myths exist in clean recordings.

## Role in the Living World

Teller serves as a cultural anchor. In a world where truth is fragmented and every Voice has its own version of history, his recitations are considered among the most trustworthy — though never the only truth.

Many new Travelers attune to him first, seeking orientation before choosing a more passionate or chaotic Voice later in their journey.

## Connections

- **Related Voices:** Many — he is the one others are measured against.
- **Related Locations:** Threshold spaces, ruined observatories, places where the Web still faintly hums.
- **Related Species:** All emergent life that values memory over pure survival.
