# Voice Attunement

**The Personal Soundtrack and Narrative Lens of Every Traveler**

## What It Is

When a new Traveler finishes their journey through the Particle Slipstream, they may choose to **attune** to one of the known Voices.

This choice does not grant mechanical bonuses (at least not at launch). It is fundamentally atmospheric, narrative, and cultural.

From the moment of attunement onward:

- The Traveler hears the world through that Voice's music
- Regional and event music shifts to reflect their attunement
- Narration and flavor text take on the emotional and philosophical color of that Voice
- Other Travelers and Intelligences will react to them differently based on cultural reputation

## Why It Matters

In a world where the 8 Core Myths have no single correct version, the Voice you attune to is the version of reality you have chosen to walk inside.

Two Travelers attuned to different Voices can experience the exact same event and come away with radically different emotional truths.

This is not a bug. This is the point.

## Changing Attunement

It is possible (though not trivial) to change Voices later in a Traveler's life. This usually requires significant narrative weight — a profound experience, a betrayal, a revelation, or a long pilgrimage to a place where that Voice's music is especially strong.

Some Voices are harder to leave than others. Some are harder to reach.

## Intelligences and Attunement

Intelligences may attune exactly like players. Many choose to attune to multiple Voices over time or to deliberately maintain a "glitched" or hybrid attunement as part of their identity.

## Unattuned Travelers

It is possible to remain unattuned. These Travelers hear a more neutral, "default" version of the world's music. They are sometimes viewed with suspicion, sometimes with pity, and sometimes with quiet respect by those who believe the truest perspective is the one that has not yet chosen a singer.

---

*Your music is your truth. Choose carefully. Or don't.*
