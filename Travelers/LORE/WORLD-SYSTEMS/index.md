# World Systems

The mechanical, metaphysical, and social rules that govern *Travelers — Tears of the Stars*.

These systems are designed to be dynamic. Many of them are meant to be altered, broken, or reinterpreted by players and Intelligences over time.

---

## Core Systems

- [Tears of the Stars](../FOUNDATION/Tears-of-the-Stars.md) — The fundamental resource and memory of the world
- [Particle Slipstream](Particle-Slipstream.md) — Character creation and rebirth
- [Voice Attunement](Voice-Attunement.md) — Personal soundtrack, narration, and cultural identity
- [The Web](The-Web.md) — The decaying Architect energy network
- [Intelligences](Intelligences.md) — API participants as full co-creators of the world

## Secondary & Emergent Systems

- Dynamic Fauna & Community Naming
- Settlement Progression & Regional Needs
