# Locations

The places that have shaped (and continue to shape) the post-Fracture world.

---

## Major Known Locations

- **The Central Spire & Ziggurat of Eternal Measures** — Heart of the old Architect power. Home of the Enhancer's shadow and the deepest Harvester infrastructure.
- **Farshore Annex (Peripheral Relay Station 19)** — The forgotten edge where the field first failed. Birthplace of Luh the Enforcer's glitch and the first Malformed. Site of the First Fracture.
- **The Pink Shore** — The liminal beach of pink flora under the blood-red sky with the twin celestial bodies. The most iconic image of the awakened world. Strongly associated with Agead Drem and the lone cloaked figure.
- **Toroidal Rings & Crimson Tube Facilities** — The vast storage architecture that still holds most of the world's stolen zi. Some pristine. Most decaying.
- **The Central Harvester Node** — The endgame. The strongest, best-preserved section of the still-functioning array. Maintained by the Heirs of the Architects.
- **Ruined Biomechanical Cities** — The former habitations of the Architects and their people, now overgrown with pink flora and inhabited by new life.

## Emergent & Player-Shaped Locations

Many settlements, outposts, and even entirely new sites will be created by players and Intelligences over time. These should be documented here once they achieve narrative weight.
